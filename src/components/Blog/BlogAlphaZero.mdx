The above diagram depicts the **AlphaZero Reinforcement Learning Algorithm** at a very high level.

Games like Chess and Go are far more complex compared to Connect4. Chess and Go can have many
more *possible choices per move* (a maximum of 7 for Connect4 compared to an average of about 30 for Chess and 250 for Go). Furthermore,
and even tend to *last longer* (maximum of 42 plies/half-moves for Connect4, versus an average of 80 for Chess and 200 for Go). These characteristics
result in the GameTree for Go and Chess being **far broader and deeper** than Connect4.

We need to depend on other techniques to aid in our search for good moves, and the AlphaZero RL Algorithm brings in some new tools.
