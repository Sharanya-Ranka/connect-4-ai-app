The above diagram depicts the **AlphaZero Reinforcement Learning Algorithm** at a very high level.

Games like Chess and Go are far more complex compared to Connect4. They have many
more *possible choices per move* (an average of about 30 for Chess and 250 for Go versus a maximum of 7 for Connect4),
and even tend to *last longer* (an average of 80 plies/half-moves for Chess and 200 for Go compared to a maximum of 42 for Connect4). These characteristics
result in the GameTree for Go and Chess being **far broader and deeper** than Connect4.

We need to depend on other techniques to aid in our search for good moves, and the AlphaZero RL Algorithm brings in some new tools.
